Questions The Assistant Can Answer

This list is for the current verified ERP POC. The strongest answers are for Projects, Billing, Purchase Orders, Vendors, Store / Material Movement, Materials, and basic Inventory.

For ambiguous questions, the assistant should ask a short clarification instead of guessing the wrong project, vendor, metric, date range, or business formula.

Questions It Should Not Guess

  • What is the company profit?
  • What is the project loss?
  • Which project is performing best?
  • What is the risk score?
  • Which vendor is most reliable?
  • Which material should we buy next?

These need verified formulas, thresholds, or business definitions. The assistant should ask for clarification or say the current ERP catalog does not safely support the KPI yet.
